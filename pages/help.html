Help

Go to the index to see all your golinks.

The index allows you to list, create, edit, and delete your golinks.

There are two easy ways to quickly access the index:

  1. Type go links (or just go + Space) in your browser's address bar, then press Enter
  2. Pin the extension in your browser's toolbar, then click its icon and select Options

Tips

The name of a golink will always be lowercase.

If you enter a golink name that doesn't yet exist you'll be forwarded to create a new golink.

You can namespace golinks with slashes (gh/repos).

To search your golinks, type a name after go + Space. Select a suggestion to open it, or press Enter to use the name as typed.
